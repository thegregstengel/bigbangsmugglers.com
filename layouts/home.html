Federation or pirate, decided by playing

Start neutral. Clean routes push you one way; contraband and first strikes push the other. Alignment sets your prices, port access, and which hulls you may fly.

Seasons of every length

Sprints and marathons, small maps and sprawling ones, each balanced for its length. Standings lock when the galaxy closes and carry forward as prestige.

Corporations

Fly as a fleet, capture and hold ports, share planets under corp policy, climb the standings under one banner.

45 hulls, five tiers deep.
