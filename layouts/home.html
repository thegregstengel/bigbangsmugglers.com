Living markets

COMBAT

PvP anywhere outside Fed space

Fighters, shields, torpedoes, hull. Win and take the cargo; lose and hope the escape pod holds.

Federation or pirate — you choose by playing

Start neutral. Clean routes push you one way; contraband and first strikes push the other. Alignment sets your prices, port access, and which hulls you're allowed to fly.

Seasons of every length

Sprints and marathons, small maps and sprawling ones, each balanced for its length. Standings lock when the galaxy closes and carry forward as permanent prestige.

Corporations

04 / Progression

The game is always watching the work.

Every real act — a trade, a kill, a scan, a bounty collected, a sector seen for the first time — goes into one action stream. Missions, goals and streaks all read from it. Nothing is self-reported, and objectives bind to actual things in the galaxy, not to descriptions of them.

Everything pays in five currencies
Credits XP Turns Alignment Standing

Turns are the important one: finishing your dailies hands back more turns, so the reward for playing well is a longer session.

STREAK

Streaks

Any day you spend a turn counts. Miss one day and it's forgiven. Your streak is the only thing that crosses season boundaries — payouts are seasonal, showing up isn't.

3 days 7 days 14 days 30 days
DAILY

Daily missions

A rotating slate — trade runs, cargo hauls, pirate hunts, convoy raids, surveys, contraband moves. Honest work nudges your alignment up; underworld work drags it down. Exploring and duelling stay neutral on purpose.

Credits · XP · bonus turns
SEASON

Season arcs

Seven long hauls that run the length of a season, from an easy opening muster up to elite gauntlets. This is where legendary gear is earned rather than rolled — the Overdrive module, the Vanguard hull, champion titles.

Up to 80k credits · 4,000 XP · 50 turns
LADDERS

Goals

Bronze → Silver → Gold ladders per category: combat, trade, exploration, economy, piracy. Progress accrues on its own as you play. Top tiers grant titles that stay in your lifetime trophy case.

Credits · XP · titles
EPIC

Epic goals

Commissioned for the impossible. Compound conditions above Gold — stat floors, standing floors, a clean record, a measured fraction of the galaxy explored. Tuned so maybe one captain a season gets there.

One epic hull · grant-only, never sold
