How It Plays

[TRADE]

Trade Routes

Three commodities: fuel, organics, equipment. Ports run a living supply-and-demand market — buy where prices are low, sell where they're high. Better ships carry more per run. Better routes mean bigger margins.

[COMBAT]

PvP Combat

Attack rival captains anywhere outside patrolled Federation space. Win and loot a cut of their credits and cargo — pirate captains loot hardest. Fighters, shields, torpedoes, and your hull decide the outcome; when it fails, there's always the escape pod.

[PLANETS]

Planet Development

Claim an unclaimed planet and grow its population. Build up six structures — factories, warehouses, habitats, shields, barracks, a citadel. Production rolls in every 4-hour cycle; a starbase caps it all off.

[BOUNTY]

Bounty Hunting

Post a price on a rival captain — anyone who defeats them collects. Faction bounties turn your wins into claims you redeem in person at the issuing faction's registry. The wanted list is always hiring.

No Two Galaxies Are the Same

Procedurally generated. Reborn every season. Your choices shape every run.

01

Federation or Pirate

Start neutral. Every action moves your standing — hunt pirates and run clean routes, or sell contraband and shoot first. Alignment changes your prices, your port access, which hulls you can fly, and whether patrols wave you through or open fire.

02

A Gradient of Danger

From the Federation core to the outer rim, every galaxy runs a danger gradient: patrolled, PvP-free Federation space; open frontier sectors; and lawless pirate pockets on the rim — where the richest planets and the black markets wait.

03

Seasons of Every Size

Every season is a freshly generated galaxy — sprints and marathons, small maps and sprawling ones, each tuned to its length. Final standings lock when the galaxy closes, and your record carries forward as permanent prestige.

04

Corporations

Form or join a corporation. Fly together as a fleet, capture and hold ports, share planets under corp policy, and climb the seasonal standings under one banner.

Your Ship. Your Rules.

Nine Lines · Five Tiers

45 purchasable hulls across nine lines. The neutral spine is open to anyone. Federation hulls ship with reinforced shields; Pirate hulls run better escape ratings and hidden compartments customs scans can't see. Six epic hulls can't be bought at all — only earned.

> Neutral: Trading, War, Balanced
> Fed: Patrol, Enforcer, Dreadnought
> Pirate: Smuggling, Raider, Corsair
> Tiers unlock as you level. Upgrade holds, shields, fighters, torpedoes

What's New in 2.0

Not an update — a re-founding. The entire game was rebuilt from the engine up, seventeen game systems were redesigned, the universe started fresh with no carried history, and the game now lives at play.bigbangsmugglers.com.

[UNIVERSE]

A Fresh Universe

A clean slate for everyone — veterans and new captains alike started from zero: new accounts, new galaxies, no carried history. The v1 record books are archived with honor; the new ones are open. Signing up takes seconds.

[ENGINE]

A New Engine

A completely new platform: instant turn resolution, galaxy generation in seconds instead of half-hours, and live balance tuning that lets every season run its own rules — different sizes, different lengths, different knobs — without app updates.

[SYSTEMS]

The Galaxy, Redesigned

A living supply-and-demand market, a full smuggling career, port capture-and-hold, planets with starbases, factions that track your standing, corporations and fleets, bounty claims, season insignia, and a real hull-damage model with escape pods.

[PLAY ANYWHERE]

Browser & Android

Play in your browser at play.bigbangsmugglers.com — no install needed — or on Android from Google Play. One account, the same galaxy, wherever you sign in.
