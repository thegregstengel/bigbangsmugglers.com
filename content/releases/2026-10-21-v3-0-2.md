- **STARNAV on web:** clicking a sector opens its card again, dragging pans without selecting anything, and search or the ★ Favorites filter shows a row of result chips you can tap to jump to that sector.
- **Header:** the collapsed strip always shows credits on one line, and the expanded XP, Shields and Hull bars have their labels and values back.
- **NPC encounters:** the NPC's tier sits on its own badge, readable in portrait.
- **Bounties:** a wanted captain refused at a capital can still reach that capital's Bounty Registry to pay off their warrant.
- **NPC Wins:** fights won against NPCs now count, shown next to PvP Wins on your stats and profile. Past wins are restored where the combat logs have them.
- **Chart fixes:** sectors filled in from your old visits no longer show who controls them today, and chart corrections made on the server now reach an open app without a reload.
