*Fixes for three reports from the 2.5.2 rollout.*

## Interface
- **The bottom tab bar is whole again.** On some Android phones, closing the keyboard left half the tab bar pushed off the bottom of the screen. While you type, the tab bar now hides and the screen rises with the keyboard. When the keyboard closes, the tab bar comes back.
- **Planet raid results.** When a planet raid was repelled, the result pop-up cut its Close button in half. It now shows in full.

## Comms
- **The Corp badge clears.** Corp messages sent while the app was in the background could leave a red badge that reading the channel never cleared, and those messages were missing from the chat. Opening Corp chat, or coming back to the app, now loads anything you missed and clears the badge.
