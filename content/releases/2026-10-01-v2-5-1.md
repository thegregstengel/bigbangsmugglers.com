*Comms fixes from launch night.*

## Comms
- **Composers stay above the keyboard on Android.** In Hails and Corp chat the message box was covered once the keyboard opened. Both now rise with it.
- **The Hail search box is usable.** It was small and sat under the keyboard. It now opens at the top of the screen with a full-size input.
- **Channel chips no longer wrap.** At larger text sizes "Leaderboards" broke onto a second line. The row now scrolls sideways when it needs to.
- **Unread counts clear when you read.** Corp chat could keep a count of 1 after you'd read everything. Reading now clears it and it stays clear.
