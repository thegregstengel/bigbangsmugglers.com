*Every fix in this release started as a player report. Thanks to SauceLord Dulak and the plague for the clear write-ups.*

## Smuggling
- **Hidden compartments now protect your sales.** Ships with hidden compartments used to hide cargo only from patrol scans; the risk of getting caught at a street sale was the same as any other hull. That risk is now cut by the compartment percentage. On The Phantom Manifest it drops 60%, so a Fed core sale goes from 22% to about 9%. The sell screen shows your real number.

## Boosters
- **XP and Credit boosters now work.** They only ever counted on mission rewards. XP boosters now apply to every source of experience. Credit boosters add 25% on top of your profit from trade and smuggling sales, and on loot, bounties, landmark caches, and mission, goal and streak rewards. They don't apply to credits taken from other players.

## Planets
- **Withdrawals use your real cargo capacity.** Cargo compressors and hull cargo bonuses now count when pulling goods from planet storage, and the withdraw screen has a Max button.

## Corporations
- **Accepting an invite actually joins you.** Invites now work on every corporation, not just invite-only ones. Inviting a captain who has already asked to join approves them on the spot, and your membership shows immediately without signing out.
- **Declined requests say so.** If a leader turns down your request, the corp list shows Declined instead of a permanent Pending, and Check Status no longer quietly files a new request.
- Leaders now see new join requests as they arrive.

## Interface
- **Long profiles can be closed again.** Captain profiles opened from the leaderboard grew taller than the screen as honors piled up, and on web the Close button was unreachable. Every pop-up is now capped to the screen, scrolls inside, and keeps Close in view. Tapping outside or pressing back closes the ones that allow it.

## Website
- **Live season standings.** bigbangsmugglers.com now shows the current season's status and standings, refreshed every four hours.

## Under the hood
- Cargo volume rules now come from the server in one place.
- Housekeeping on the agent tooling in the repo.
