The first weekend of 2.0 brought real players, and real players find real bugs. Two patches went out in response — thank you to the captains who filed reports. You know who you are, and soon the galaxy will too.

## v2.0.2

- **Defense contracts now mean it.** A purchased contract grants full combat immunity for its window — NPC pirate and patrol ambushes are suppressed, not just player attacks. If you bought two hours of peace to trade, you get two hours of peace.
- **Honest trade numbers.** Your cargo now tracks what you actually paid for it (weighted average, taxes and fees included), so sell screens report true profit — including negative profit when you dump at a loss. Fee and tax estimates now come from the live market instead of guesses.
- **The feed tells the story.** Combat outcomes, port and planet captures, arrests at dock, and more now appear with real names and details in the galaxy feed and the "while you were away" recap, instead of generic labels.
- Fixed a crash when expanding Recent Sectors, and added an app-wide safety net so a single broken screen can no longer blank the whole app.

## v2.0.3

- **Ship tiers display correctly.** Owned ships showed "Tier 1" no matter the hull — and that phantom tier was blocking warp drive installation on tier-2+ ships. Your Tristar Hauler is, and always was, Tier 3; now the game admits it.
- **Fixed a crash when engaging an enemy** from the sector view.
- **Engage intel is real.** Enemy fighters, shields, and torpedoes now show actual values in the engage dialog (win estimates were being computed from zeroed stats).
- **New: the Hull Whisperer insignia** — a season honor granted by the GM to players whose bug reports shaped the season. It burns with the season, like all insignia. Earn it the hard way: break our game and tell us how.

Android: version 2.0.3 is rolling out on Google Play. Web players at [play.bigbangsmugglers.com](https://play.bigbangsmugglers.com) are already on it.
