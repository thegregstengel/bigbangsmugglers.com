*Dominion arrives as a preview, and next season's rules are locked in. Update the Android app from Google Play to see the Dominion boards.*

## Dominion (preview)
**What is Dominion?** Dominion measures how much of the galaxy you actually control. XP shows how hard you've played and credits show how rich you are. Dominion rewards holding ground: ports you've taken, planets you've built up, starbases you've raised. It builds up over time, so an empire held for weeks outscores a quick grab. Corps score together, so a corp that holds territory as a team can top the board without the richest captain.

- **How it scores:** every 4 hours you earn points for what you hold: 500 per port, 20 per planet structure level, and 1,000 per starbase. A bare rock scores nothing.
- **New Dominion boards** on the Leaderboards tab: Captains or Corps, for the whole season or the last 30 days. Hidden planets still count, and no locations are shown.
- **This season it's a preview.** It doesn't change your podium or awards on AstraPrime. Next season it counts.

## Next season's rules (AstraPrime plays on unchanged)
- **One season score:** XP, credits, kills and Dominion together decide the podium and the overall board.
- **New awards:** Galactic Dominion (top captain, plus every member of the top corp), Legend Leader, Protection Racket and Colonist.
- **Trade XP comes from profit.** You earn XP when you sell, based on what you made. Buying and dumping at the same port earns nothing.
- **A ladder that fits the season.** Levels are paced so a steady captain reaches level 100 about 80% of the way through the season.
- **Epic goals scale** with season length and with how many captains are playing.
- **Gates reset to the new ladder:** tech modules, mercenaries, planet mine-laying and posting in Galaxy chat.
- **Bigger PvP takes:** the cash a winner can take grows with the loser's hull tier, from 10k at T1 to 500k at T7. It still comes from the wallet only; banked credits stay safe.
- **Trade-ins refund half your upgrades:** trading a hull in returns 50% of what its stat upgrades cost.
