*Every text input now clears the keyboard.*

## Interface
- **Text boxes stay above the keyboard everywhere.** Trade and bank amounts, bounty postings, ship and planet names, sector notes, galaxy posts, corp chat, hails, and the confirmation fields in pop-ups all sat under the Android keyboard. They now rise with it, on every screen and in every pop-up.
- **Chat composers, done properly this time.** The v2.5.1 fix for Hails and corp chat under-corrected by the height of the header. The avoider now measures its real position on screen.
