*The second wave of the economy changes. Ports are worth holding, planets show what they earn, and the outlaw life gets a way back. Update the Android app from Google Play to get the new screens.*

## Ports
- **Held ports pay.** A port your corp holds now earns a protection levy every 4 hours, and its upkeep is lower, so a held port makes money even when nobody trades there.
- **You can take a port solo.** Neutral garrisons regrow 10% per cycle instead of resetting fully every 4 hours, so a lone captain can wear one down over a day. A one-member corp can hold one port.
- **Sieges pay properly.** Winning a siege pays in proportion to the port's size, and razing pays more. A razed port can't be razed again for 7 days.
- **Fortify your ports.** Holders can buy extra garrison fighters.
- **Lawful stakes.** Lawful captains can buy a stake in a port (5% of its stock value) and earn a share of its levy, with up to 4 stakeholders per port. Pirates who capture or raze the port wipe the stakes out.
- **Port P&L.** The port screen shows the holder's levy, upkeep, tax income and net per day.

## Planets
- **Export pays on what you store.** The export dividend rises from 30 to 45 per unit, but only on output that lands in storage. A full warehouse stops paying, so haul it, sell it, or expand.
- **Trading posts sell to the port in their own sector** at that port's live price. Half the till banks itself every cycle.
- **Citadels** now add 15% production per level, up from 10%.
- **Barracks make torpedoes.** Load them, and spare garrison fighters, onto your landed ship.
- **Payback on every button:** each build shows what it adds per day and how long it takes to pay for itself.

## Outlaws & bounties
- **Heat cools off.** Faction heat now halves every 3 days with no new offence. Warrants shrink with it and lapse at zero. A faction only shoots on sight while your heat is above 2.
- **Deep-Federation runs pay more:** contraband sold deep in Federation space now earns up to 2× the outer-rim rate, with the matching risk.

## Mines
- **Mines bite.** A big minefield now triggers up to 10 mines per entry instead of 3. Starter ships get a one-time survival floor.
- **Recover your own mines,** and Patrol-line hulls (T3+) can sweep hostile fields with torpedoes.

## Ships & prices
- **Upgrades are cheaper:** maxing out a starter ship costs about 81k instead of 119k.
- **Neutral hulls cost 10% less** than faction hulls.
- **Provisions get pricier as you buy:** up to 20 per refill cycle, with each one costing more than the last, as a luxury on top of your turn bank.
- **Missions:** 6 rotating missions a day, with credit rewards that scale with your level.

## While you were gone
- The away summary now shows turns banked, planet income, a full-storage warning, port levy and stake dividends, alerts, heat cooling and expiring missions.
