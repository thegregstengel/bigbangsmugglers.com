*Something to chase past level 100, and things worth spending a fortune on. Update the Android app from Google Play to get the new screens.*

## Legend ranks unlock real things
- Every 100,000 XP past level 100 is a Legend rank, and now they open content:
  - **Legend 5:** Tier 7 hulls.
  - **Legend 10:** planet structures at level 8.
  - **Legend 20:** the rarest luxury stock.
  - **Legend 40:** an exclusive title (Eternal Guardian, Legend Eternal, or Undying Scourge) and the epithet *the Ascendant*.
- Your Captain's Log shows the Legend ladder once you're close.

## Bigger ships
- **Tier 6 and Tier 7 hulls** on every line: T6 from level 85 (625k, about 480 holds), T7 from Legend 5 (1.5M, about 720 holds). The neutral line stays 10% cheaper, and Federation hulls keep their extra shields.
- **Epic hulls grow with you.** Your epic becomes Tier 6 at level 85 and Tier 7 at Legend 5, and keeps its signature ability. Upgrades you've bought carry over.
- **Writ of the Council fixed and boosted:** correct shields, a stronger sensor edge, and +10% defence. **Cartographer's Dream** now scans one ring further.
- **Lost your epic?** Re-commission it for 250k, once per season.

## Bigger planets
- **Structure levels 6–8:** level 6 at captain level 80, level 7 at level 100, and level 8 at Legend 10. Starbase requirements are unchanged.

## Luxuries
- **Full-Hold Cloak (25M, black market):** customs scans find nothing in your hold for 24 hours.
- **Forged Credentials (50M, black market):** dock at one Stardock for 7 days, whatever your reputation. A live warrant still gets you arrested.
- **Starbase Hull-Swap Bay (40M, Stardock):** switch hulls at your own starbase and take your cargo with you.
- **Second Module Slot (100M, Stardock, Legend 20):** fit a second Cargo Compressors or Shield Booster.
- **Longer cloaks:** 8 hours for 15k and 24 hours for 60k, alongside the 2-hour cloak. StarNav 4 still sees through all of them.

## Bank bonds
- **Lock credits in a 7-day bond** and earn up to 2%. Bonds are paid from a pool fed by fees and fines across the galaxy, never created from nothing. Cashing in early costs 1% and forfeits the yield.
