*The first wave of the economy changes we asked you about on Discord. Everything below is live for everyone now, Android included. A few new screens reach the Android app with the next app update; they're marked.*

## Turns
- **Turns bank now.** You still get +250 every 4 hours, but unused turns no longer vanish at the next refill. They carry over, up to 1,000.
- Log in once a day and you'll find up to 1,000 turns waiting, four times what a single session got before.
- Everyone starts the new system with at least 250 in the bank.
- Losing your ship empties your whole turn bank.
- Provisions are unchanged: 5 per 4-hour refill.

## Smuggling
- **Smaller contraband loads.** Black tech is now 30 per purchase; stolen equipment and illicit organics are 50. Smuggling still pays more than honest trading, just not four times more.
- **Fences buy back below their price.** Buying at a fence and selling straight back now loses money. Fences are the safe place to dump hot cargo; the profit is in the risky sales at ports.

## Trading
- **Federation, pirate and black-market ports now feel your volume** like every other port. Hammer one route and its prices move; leave it alone and they recover.

## Planets
- **Faster start.** New claims begin with 150 colonists and grow faster, filling up in about 5 days instead of 10.
- **Habitats finally pay.** Higher habitat levels used to *lower* output. Now every level raises it, by up to about 49% at level 5.
- **Planet locations are secret.** Claim and capture stories no longer say which sector. You find planets by flying there.

## Ships
- **Trade in your old hull** when you buy a new one, for 64% of its value, and bring your drives along for a 10% refit fee. You can still choose to keep the old hull. *(The trade-in option shows in the Android app with the next app update. Until then, buying on Android works as before.)*

## Your captain
- **Rank titles all the way to level 100,** on three tracks by alignment: lawful, neutral and outlaw. Yes, there's an Evil Scourge.
- **A badge every 10 levels.**
- **Legend ranks** past level 100.
- **Iron Will** can now be earned; your longest streak is finally tracked.

## New players
*(Web now, Android with the next app update.)*
- You start on the Nav screen.
- A Captain's Log shows your next unlocks and how far away they are.
- A first-trade card plans a profitable run near you.
- The Getting Started checklist actually completes.

## Bounties
- **The Wanted card shows the details:** your heat, what the next offence would cost, whether that faction shoots on sight, and where to settle. *(Web now, Android with the next app update.)*
