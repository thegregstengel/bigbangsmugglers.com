Bug fixes since v3.1.0.

### StarNav
- Layer chip selections (Ports, Planets, Control, and the rest) now stick when you move sectors instead of resetting to all-on.

### Combat / limpets
- Only one limpet per planter per hull. Scrubbing clears that latch so a tracker cannot stay attached after a successful scrub.

### Corps / navigation
- Warping to a corp planet from the Corp Planets list refreshes sector, Dock, and Planet views so you can dock or manage the planet without tapping Nav first.

### Combat / feed
- Planet raid reports name the attacker for the defender.
- When a corp mate's minefield is hit, corp mates get a field report (not only the planter).

### Planets / cargo
- On planet cargo transfer, the quantity field stays above the keyboard instead of hiding under it.
