Short definitions, alphabetical. Each entry links to the page that explains
the mechanic properly.

## A–C

**Alignment.** Which side you're on: one score from −1,000 (Syndicate) to
+1,000 (Federation), starting at zero. Sets prices at faction ports, docking
rights at the capitals, and which faction hulls you may fly.
[Factions, Alignment & Standing](/guide/reputation-factions/).

**Anchor price.** The price a port archetype charges when its stock sits
exactly at baseline. Stock deviation and trade pressure move the real price
off it. [Trading](/guide/trading/).

**Archetype (port).** A port's fixed personality: which commodities it
favors, which of the eleven services it runs. Stardock, Merchant Exchange,
Federation Port, Trading Port, Fuel Depot, Agricultural Port, Tech Port,
Mining Port, Research Port, Pirate Base, Black Market, Pirate Haven.
[Ports & Services](/guide/starports/).

**Archetype (ship).** Trading, War or Balanced: the hull's balance of holds
against combat stats. Combined with a line and a tier it names one of the 45
hulls. [Ships](/guide/ships/).

**Bank.** Credits that cannot be looted, seized, or spent on most things.
Deposit at a `banking` service. [Turns, Credits & Basics](/guide/gameplay/).

**Bounty claim.** The redeemable ticket you earn by beating a captain who
carries a faction bounty. Cash it at the issuing registry, in person.
[Bounties](/guide/bounties/).

**Capital.** The Stardock (Federation) and the Pirate Haven (Syndicate). The
only two docks with a bounty office; each arrests wanted captains at the
door. [Ports & Services](/guide/starports/).

**Changed in.** The release versions listed at the top of every guide page:
the builds that altered the rules on that page.

**Citadel.** The planet structure that multiplies garrison cap, production
and defense. Requires Factory and Shield Generator at level 3.
[Planets](/guide/planets/).

**Cloak.** A consumable that hides your ship for a fixed two hours. Breaks
the moment you act. Seen through at StarNav 4.
[Scanners, Cloaks & Intel](/guide/scanners-intel/).

**Contraband.** Illicit Organics, Stolen Equipment and Black Tech: goods in
a separate cargo namespace that only fences and premium sales can move.
[Smuggling & Contraband](/guide/smuggling/).

**Corp bank.** A corporation's shared wallet, fed by held-port taxes and
member deposits. [Corporations & Fleets](/guide/corporations-fleets/).

**Cost basis.** The weighted average you paid for the cargo in your holds.
The game's profit readouts are measured against it.
[Trading](/guide/trading/).

**Customs scan.** A patrol encounter's search of your holds. Clean scans pay
alignment; dirty ones seize the overflow above your hidden allowance.
[Smuggling & Contraband](/guide/smuggling/).

## D–L

**Defense contract.** Purchased PvP immunity, two hours per contract,
stacking. Covers NPC ambushes too. [Combat](/guide/combat/).

**Epic hull.** One of six ships that are never sold, only granted by an epic
goal ladder. Dies with the season. [Ships](/guide/ships/).

**Escape pod.** What happens when your hull reaches zero: a respawn at
Sector 0 or, with a starbase, a choice. [Combat](/guide/combat/).

**Events.** Dated missions: dailies, season arcs, GM drops. No accept step;
progress accrues from ordinary play. [Missions, Goals & Streaks](/guide/missions/).

**Fence.** A `blackmarket` port that buys and sells contraband with no
detection roll. [Smuggling & Contraband](/guide/smuggling/).

**Fleet.** Corp members flying together; a fleet strike pools their power
against one target. [Corporations & Fleets](/guide/corporations-fleets/).

**Garrison.** A planet's fighters. Staffed with Security Personnel, grown by
Barracks, capped by Citadel level. [Planets](/guide/planets/).

**Goals.** Permanent Bronze / Silver / Gold ladders per category that run
all season. [Missions, Goals & Streaks](/guide/missions/).

**Hidden holds.** The fraction of your cargo volume a customs scan cannot
see: 25% on pirate-line hulls, more with hidden-compartment signatures.
[Smuggling & Contraband](/guide/smuggling/).

**Hull.** Both the ship itself and its structural hit points beneath the
shields. Shields spill to hull at ×1.5; zero hull is destruction.
[Combat](/guide/combat/).

**Immunity (PvP).** A window during which you cannot be attacked and cannot
act aggressively either. Granted by being attacked, dying, or buying a
defense contract. [Combat](/guide/combat/).

**Insignia.** Earn-only season marks: a ship prefix, an epithet, a named
sector. They burn with the season. [Progression](/guide/progression/).

**Landmark.** One of three generated points of interest per galaxy, worth
+100 XP on first visit. [Navigation & Travel](/guide/navigation/).

**Level ladder.** The season's pinned level curve. Gates are described in
this guide by depth (early, mid, late) because the numbers differ per season.
[Progression](/guide/progression/).

**Limpet tracker.** A device planted in a sector that latches silently onto
the next foreign ship and reports its position for 48 hours.
[Ordnance, Limpets & Beacons](/guide/deployables/).

**Line.** Neutral, Federation or Pirate: the faction flavor of a hull.
Federation lines carry +10% shields; pirate lines carry +0.10 escape and
hidden holds. [Ships](/guide/ships/).

## M–R

**Merchant Guild.** The third standing faction, replacing 1.x trader
reputation. A ±10% price band at neutral ports.
[Factions, Alignment & Standing](/guide/reputation-factions/).

**Prestige.** Cross-season tiers earned from lifetime statistics. One of the
few things that survives a season end. [Seasons](/guide/seasons/).

**Pressure (trade).** A per-unit multiplier that builds as a port is traded
and decays while it idles. [Trading](/guide/trading/).

**Raid.** An attack on a planet's garrison for a cut of its storage and the
owner's wallet. Empty the garrison and you can capture it.
[Planets](/guide/planets/).

**Region.** Six distance bands from Sector 0: `fed_core`, `fed_space`,
`inner`, `middle`, `outer`, `outer_rim`. Drives spawn rates, smuggling heat
and production bonuses. [The Galaxy & Territory](/guide/galaxy-territory/).

**Retinue.** Mercenaries hired at a tavern on a 48-hour contract. They fight
NPCs alongside you and desert if unpaid. [NPCs & Encounters](/guide/npcs/).

**Role.** A specialization tier earned by volume in one activity (Smuggler,
Explorer, and so on), each with a small permanent bonus.
[Progression](/guide/progression/).

## S–Z

**Season.** A freshly generated galaxy with a player cap and an end date;
the container for everything you own. [Seasons](/guide/seasons/).

**Sector 0.** The Stardock's sector, the safest place in the galaxy and
where every escape pod lands by default.
[The Galaxy & Territory](/guide/galaxy-territory/).

**Siege.** A corporation's attack on a port's garrison, three turns per
attempt. Win and capture it, or raze it.
[Corporations & Fleets](/guide/corporations-fleets/).

**Standing.** What a faction owes you: three separate scores (Federation,
Syndicate, Merchant Guild) earned by service and decaying 1% a day.
[Factions, Alignment & Standing](/guide/reputation-factions/).

**Starbase.** The capstone planet structure: respawn priority, a private
vault, a corp ship pool. 1.5M credits and every structure at level 5.
[Planets](/guide/planets/).

**StarNav 2002.** The passive scanner. Five levels; range caps at three hops
and higher levels resolve more detail.
[Scanners, Cloaks & Intel](/guide/scanners-intel/).

**Streak.** Consecutive days with at least one turn spent. One missed day is
forgiven. The count survives season boundaries.
[Missions, Goals & Streaks](/guide/missions/).

**Territory.** The axis the law cares about, separate from region:
Federation territory (no PvP, no deployables), pirate territory (the
outermost 8%), and unincorporated space between.
[The Galaxy & Territory](/guide/galaxy-territory/).

**Tesseract.** The endgame drive: an instant jump to any visited sector for
a flat 25 turns. [Navigation & Travel](/guide/navigation/).

**Tick (world).** The fixed four-hour infrastructure schedule: planet
production, restock, upkeep, garrison regrowth, ordnance decay.
[Turns, Credits & Basics](/guide/gameplay/).

**Trading post.** A planet structure that sells stored production to
visiting captains and accumulates credits in a robbable till.
[Planets](/guide/planets/).

**Truce.** A protection window after a claim or capture during which the
planet or port cannot be attacked. [Planets](/guide/planets/).

**Turn.** The unit of action. Moving, trading, fighting and deploying each
cost one; you get a full allowance every cycle and nothing carries over.
[Turns, Credits & Basics](/guide/gameplay/).

**Wallet.** Credits on your ship: spendable anywhere, lootable in PvP,
seizable at a capital. [Turns, Credits & Basics](/guide/gameplay/).

**Wanted.** Carrying a faction warrant. Everyone can see the price, patrols
shoot on sight, and the issuing capital arrests you at the dock.
[Bounties](/guide/bounties/).

**Warp drive.** Turns hops into jumps: `ceil(hops ÷ level)` turns to any
visited sector along an existing route. [Navigation & Travel](/guide/navigation/).

**Wormhole.** A link that skips distance. Stable transit bypasses mines and
hazards; an unstable exit does not. [Navigation & Travel](/guide/navigation/).
