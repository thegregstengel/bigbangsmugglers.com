A quick follow-up patch, same day.

- **Your StarNav 2002 works.** The long-range sector sweep was stuck at "Scanning…" forever — the display was never connected to the new engine. It now renders the full picture: connected sector routes, territory colors, hostile contact markers, and your own sector at the center of the sweep. Higher StarNav levels reveal more detail at greater range, as intended.
- Scan intel is honest: sectors your device can't fully resolve show as unknown rather than empty.
- If a sweep ever fails, you'll see a retry button — not an eternal spinner.

Fitting that the fix ships the same week StarNav installs themselves were unblocked. The device earns its 100,000 credits now.

Android: version 2.0.5 is rolling out on Google Play. Web players are already on it.
