Day two of launch week, and the galaxy just got a lot louder.

## The Feed Tells the Story

Until today, the galaxy feed only carried system notices — battles, land grabs, and arrivals were happening all around you, invisibly. No more:

- **Battle reports.** Every PvP engagement now posts publicly — "X destroyed Y in sector N," or drove them off, or watched them run. The ⚔️ combat lane from v1 is back.
- **Ship commissionings.** When a captain commissions a tier-3+ hull, the galaxy hears about it.
- **Land and war.** Planet claims, port captures, and ports shaking off their occupiers all post publicly now — several of these were being written with your name on them and delivered to no one.
- **New captains and season honors** appear for everyone, and feed entries carry proper categories again.

## Every Ship Is Now Its Own Ship

A sharp-eyed captain noticed two ship classes with identical stats. The audit found it ran deeper: the Federation line was a stat-for-stat clone of the neutral line. As of today:

- **Federation hulls carry +10% base shields** — the doctrine is now Federation = shields, Pirate = escape and hidden compartments, Neutral = baseline.
- All **45 purchasable hulls have provably unique stats**, and an automated test now makes it impossible to ever ship a duplicate again.
- The T4 **Starlane Guardian's** sensor edge now actually improves on T3 (reveals cloaked ships at StarNav 2+), and the **Star Galleon** finally gets its identity: +10% cargo capacity.

Your owned ships keep everything they have — ceilings only went up.

## Fixes

- Warp travel was blocked for everyone by a false "not enough turns" — fixed.
- The StarNav 2002 install button was missing from the starport — fixed, and drive pricing now comes straight from the live catalog.
- The hangar showed "Paid 0 cr · Value 0 cr" — ships now record what you actually paid, and Value shows the real trade-in quote.
