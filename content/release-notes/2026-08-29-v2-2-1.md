*A bug sweep from captain field reports.*

## Combat & missions

- Defeating an NPC that ambushed you — mid-move or mid-warp — now counts toward mission objectives and goal ladders (*Scourge of Pirates*, *Terror of the Lanes*). Previously only fights you initiated counted, even though the kill showed in your stats.
- *Made of Money* now progresses: bank deposits were never counted toward the goal, for anyone.

## Ship & inventory

- Supply shop purchases (Sentry Drones, mines, turrets, buoys, cloak fields) appear on the Ship screen's Ordnance card immediately.
- Recalling your own nav beacon returns it to inventory, as the confirmation promised. Destroying someone else's still grants nothing.

## Navigation

- The Nav tab lists every ship in your sector, including offline captains (shown dimmed, still targetable) — it now agrees with sector scans and StarNav counts.

## Stats & leaderboards

- The Settings player stats card shows real season numbers — K/D, missions, goals, bounties, trades — instead of zeros.
- Planets claimed, distance traveled, and streak milestones are now tracked, count toward lifetime career totals, and the planets season award is winnable for the first time.
- Overall leaderboard scores are labeled with XP and credits.

*Counting starts from this release — earlier kills and deposits show in your stats but predate the counters.*
