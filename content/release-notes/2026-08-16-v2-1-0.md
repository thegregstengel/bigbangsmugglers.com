*Everything shipped since v2.0.9 — a launch-month cleanup rolled into one release.*

## Out of beta

The beta labels are gone, the app store link points where it should, and the game now has a proper credits screen. Thanks for flying with us this far.

## Combat & mines

- Minefields now name the captain who laid them — in the moment, and in the feed.
- Mine strikes are reported as attacks, not bad weather.
- Escape pods work the way you'd expect: a pod is a respawn, defending yourself earns the protection it should, and post-battle immunity actually covers everything it claims to.
- A rare double-report on NPC kills and a bounty-payout accounting bug are fixed.

## Ports & trading

- The Port and Starport screens are now one Station Directory — one dock, one door.
- Bounty offices live at the faction capitals, where they belong.
- Per-transaction purchase caps are gone.
- Planet trading posts got a real storefront, and your cargo's cost basis follows it properly through storage and sales.

## Quality of life

- Victims of an attack are told about it.
- Filter chips wrap instead of scrolling.
- Sector headcounts count the captains you can actually see.
- Admin announcements can now reach you in-game as a toast.

## Seasons ahead

Groundwork shipped for seasons of any length — sprints to marathons — each tuned so progression fits its span. Current galaxies are unchanged; you'll see this in action when the next season begins.

## Under the hood

Platform hardening, deployment security, real production monitoring, and config safety rails in the admin console. Less visible, more reliable.
