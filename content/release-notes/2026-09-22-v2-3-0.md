*Most of this release came straight from the Discord. Thank you to everyone who reported, tested and argued about it.*

## Planets
- **Auto-collect.** Each production cycle's output now goes straight into your planet's storage, up to its cap. No more flying around every cycle to press Collect. If a planet's storage fills up, the planet screen and your "while you were away" summary tell you so.
- **Planet XP.** Building structures now earns experience. The amount scales with the structure and the level you reach: a level-1 warehouse pays 60 XP, and a level-5 citadel pays 900. Colonizing pays 1 XP per worker.
- **Fixed:** missions that asked you to upgrade a planet could never complete.
- **Fixed:** leaving a season now releases your planets for others to claim.
- **Fixed:** collecting right after a cycle boundary could skip a cycle's production.

## Mines
- Each captain can now deploy up to **500 mines** across the galaxy, up from 50.
- Sectors where you own a planet allow 50 extra.

## Smuggling rebalance
- **Market-priced contraband.** Black Market prices now follow the local market instead of a flat rate. Selling back to the same fence earns a modest, safe profit. The big money is still in lawful space, at real risk.
- **100 per purchase.** You can buy up to 100 units of any contraband type in one purchase.
- **Hidden compartments stay hidden.** When a patrol catches you over your hidden allowance, it now takes only the overflow. The fine is based on what that cargo cost you.
- **NPC traders** now charge more than a Black Market for contraband, never less.

## Alignment
- **Jettison workers is live.** Dump mining workers from your hold for −10 alignment each. It's the fast road toward the pirate side.

## Combat
- **Defenders loot.** Beat someone who attacked you and you take a cut of their wallet. Banked credits stay safe.
- **Bounties pay both ways.** If a wanted captain attacks you and loses, you collect their bounty.
- **No self-sieges.** Corps can't siege their own ports.
- **No sieges under immunity.** Captains under immunity can't siege ports or plant limpets.
- **Cloaks break when you act.** Sieges, raids, Black Market deals, banking and ship purchases now drop your cloak.
- **Fixed:** equipped hull plating was increasing hull damage instead of reducing it.
- **Fixed:** mines could hit your replacement ship right after a fatal ambush.

## Navigation
- **Warps charge their full cost up front.** If you're stopped partway, the turns for the hops you didn't fly come back.
- **Fixed:** warp interrupts with no contact.
- **Fixed:** arriving hidden after a wormhole jump while landed on a planet.
- Warp exploration now counts toward missions and goals.

## Fixes you reported
- **Corp bank.** Deposits and withdrawals now show up instantly for the whole crew.
- **Tavern intel.** Results no longer run off the screen.
- **Buying a ship.** Hulls with bonus cargo space no longer destroy cargo that would have fit.
- **Prices you're shown now match what you're charged**, including StarNav and drive faction discounts, trade fees, and the port price board.
- **Screens now show real numbers.** Wormhole destinations are real, ambush recaps no longer invent "50% even odds", and planet production rates are correct.
- **Corps.** Rosters update live, and you no longer appear offline while connected.

## Under the hood
- **Security hardening** around docking and port services.
- **Console improvements:** bulk bans, and version updates from the Broadcast desk.
- **Cleanup:** idle, never-played seats now free themselves.
- **Many smaller consistency and race-condition fixes.**
