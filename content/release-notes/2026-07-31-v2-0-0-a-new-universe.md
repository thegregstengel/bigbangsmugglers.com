v2.0.0 is not an update — it's a re-founding. The entire game has been rebuilt from the engine up, the universe starts fresh, and the game now lives at **play.bigbangsmugglers.com**.

## A Fresh Universe

Big Bang Smugglers 2.0 launches with a clean slate. Everyone — veterans and new captains alike — starts from zero: new accounts, new galaxies, no carried history. The years of v1 seasons are archived with honor, and the new record books open today. Sign up takes seconds with Google or email.

## A New Engine

The platform under the game is completely new: a real-time server core with instant turn resolution, galaxy generation in seconds instead of half-hours, and live game-balance tuning that lets seasons run different rules without app updates. You'll feel it as speed — everything from moving to trading to combat resolves faster.

## The Galaxy, Redesigned

Seventeen game systems were reviewed and rebuilt for 2.0:

- **A living market** — prices move with real supply and demand at every port. Trade routes are discovered, not memorized.
- **The smuggling career** — contraband, fences, black-market premiums, customs scans, bribes, and cloaks. High risk, high margin.
- **Port capture-and-hold** — sieges, garrisons, corp-owned ports that skim taxes, and the fights to take them back.
- **Planets and starbases** — claim worlds, build a six-structure economy, raise a garrison, and cap it with a starbase.
- **Factions with teeth** — the Federation, the pirates, and the Merchant Guild track your standing; prices, docking rights, hulls, and arrest warrants follow it.
- **A heavier level spine** — levels gate ships, structures, and sieges; every XP source matters across a 12-week season.
- **Corporations, fleets, and bounties** — coordinated strikes, bounty claims redeemed in person at the registry, and a Most Wanted board.
- **Season insignia** — earn-only marks (a ship prefix, an epithet, a named sector) that burn with the season. Flex while it lasts.
- **Hull damage and escape pods** — shields spill to hull, destruction is real, and the pod ride home comes with choices.

## On Your Phone and In Your Browser

The web game is live now at **play.bigbangsmugglers.com** — no install needed. The Android app 2.0 update rolls out on Google Play; existing installs update in place.

## The Fine Print

v1's web client has retired. The v1 Android app will continue to work briefly but no new seasons will run there — update when prompted. Security hardening and infrastructure improvements ship throughout.
